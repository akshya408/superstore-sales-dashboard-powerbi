# 📊 Power BI Dashboard Storyboard: Superstore Sales Performance
🟦 1. Dashboard Title Page
Title: Superstore Sales Dashboard

Subtitle: Sales Overview by Region, Category, and Time

Date Range: Jan 2022 – Dec 2023

Filter Options:

Region

Category

📈 2. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
Visual: Line Chart

X-axis: Month-Year

Y-axis: Total Sales

Purpose: Show sales trends and seasonal patterns.

Insight Example:

📌 "Sales peaked in November 2022, likely due to holiday season promotions."

📊 3. Sales by Region (Bar Chart)
Visual: Clustered Bar Chart

X-axis: Region

Y-axis: Total Sales

Color: Conditional formatting to highlight top region

Purpose: Compare total sales performance by geographic area.

Insight Example:

📌 "The West region had the highest sales, contributing over 35% of total revenue."

🍩 4. Sales by Category (Donut Chart)
Visual: Donut Chart

Legend: Category

Values: Sales

Purpose: Identify product category performance.

Insight Example:

📌 "Technology category led in sales, showing consistent demand across all regions."

🧭 5. Interactive Slicer Panel
Visual: Slicers

Slicer 1: Region

Slicer 2: Category

Purpose: Allow user to dynamically filter data across visuals.

🎯 6. KPI Cards (Optional)
KPIs:

Total Sales

Average Sales per Order

Highest Performing Region

🎨 7. Color Scheme (For Clarity)
Technology: Blue

Office Supplies: Orange

Furniture: Green

Top Region: Highlight with a darker or vivid color
